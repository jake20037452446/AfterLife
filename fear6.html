Letters from Survivors

Daniel Brooks — For His Girlfriend

Age: 26

Date of death: October 14, 2024

Story: Daniel suffered from PTSD, nightmares, and flashbacks after surviving a terrifying zombie attack. He became emotionally numb, pushed his girlfriend away, and struggled to explain his pain.

Dear Emily,

I'm sorry I became so distant. The nightmares and memories never seemed to stop, and sometimes I couldn't feel anything at all. I loved you, but I didn't know how to explain what was happening inside me. I wish I'd known how to ask for help.

— Daniel

Michael Carter — For His Family

Age: 31

Date of death: March 8, 2023

Story: Michael witnessed terrible things during the zombie outbreak. Haunted by flashbacks and constant fear, he isolated himself from his family and struggled to feel connected to anyone.

Dear Mom and Dad,

I miss the person I used to be. The memories follow me everywhere, and I don't know how to explain why I've become so cold and distant. I wish I could tell you what I'm going through instead of hiding it.

— Michael
